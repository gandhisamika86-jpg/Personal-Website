Senior Year Capstone Project @ Genentech.

▪ Overview
Developing a force-dose assist device that reduces subcutaneous and ocular injection forces for high-concentration biologic drug formulations.
01
Section 1
Problem Statement

New biologic drug formulations increasingly need to be highly concentrated to enable extended-release dosing, fewer, less frequent injections for the patient. But that concentration comes at a cost: more concentrated biologics are also more viscous, and viscous fluid is fundamentally harder to push through a needle. That tension shows up directly at the point of care, where high injection forces make these next-generation drug solutions genuinely difficult to deliver through the small-gauge needles patients actually use.

The goal of this capstone is to design a force-dose assist device, a tool that interfaces with an off-the-shelf syringe, such as the standard 1 mL BD syringe, and physically reduces the force a patient or provider has to apply to complete the injection. Solving this well is what stands between a promising high-concentration formulation and a therapy patients can actually self-administer.
